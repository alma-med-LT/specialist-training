Onboarding Clients

In-region clients: click the green button. Out-of-region clients: click the grey paper airplane button just above it.

To acknowledge something a client said and still send the standard onboarding info: type your own message and send it, then click the grey button, then copy the second paragraph of the onboarding message (usually the SNAP question, soup kitchen question, or “let me know how it goes” line) and send that separately.

Example: sending resources with a follow-up note about interest in comedores (soup kitchens).

Out-of-region clients

These steps are for clients whose zip code shows in red.

  1. Check the driving distance using the Out-of-Region Decision Helper or Google Maps. We go by miles, not travel time.
  2. More than 50 driving miles from a region we serve: process as out of region.
  3. Within 50 driving miles: review the auto-referred resources. The client needs at least 3 resources, each at 70% confidence or higher. If any fall short, refer qualifying resources manually.
  4. 3 qualifying resources found: send the “Unreliable/Just Outside Coverage Area” template and clear the flag. No follow-up needed.
  5. Can't find 3 qualifying resources, even after searching manually: process as out of region.
Example: Mary Jackson (Loganville, GA) — Unreliable + Call Ahead templates combined into one message, with the resource's distance and reviews shown for reference.

If the client replies that they want the info, onboard them with the grey paper airplane button.

If the referred resources are more than 10 minutes away (driving or walking), add the “Call Ahead” template to the same message.

  • No zip code: always make sure every address has one, so the client gets the correct nearby resources. Same process — edit pen, space bar, choose the complete option.

  • No building or house number: send the “Building Number” template to ask for it.

  • Client wants to use a P.O. box: send the “Address Follow Up” template.
Example: a client offers a P.O. box. Our system can't use it, so ask for a full address, nearest cross streets, or a nearby school/business instead.
  • Client gives a new address to replace an old one: remove the old address completely before adding the new one. Replacing in place won't clear out the old resources and will confuse the client.

  • Weird-looking address: correct the format using the space bar trick. Sometimes this turns an out-of-region address into an in-region one, which can automatically add more pantries.
Example: correcting a strange-looking address format using the space bar trick.
Per the official SOP: if you've corrected the address format and the zip still shows red (out-of-region) even though the client is genuinely within one of our service regions, don't keep trying to fix it yourself — post in #research_ops with the client's Platform link, the zip code(s) in question, and what you're asking for (e.g. “please add zip XXXXX to the database”). The space bar trick is for fixing typos/formatting, not for getting a genuinely unsupported zip added.

When referred resources are more than 10 minutes away (driving or walking) for an out-of-region client, also send the “Call Ahead” template. No follow-up needed since it's an out-of-region (red zip) client.

6-Month Reintegration & Reconfirmation

When a client hasn't been active in a while, our system checks in automatically. If they confirm they still need help, we send the 6 Month Address Confirmation template. If they confirm they're at the same address, make sure the address is complete, turn the bell on, and hit Submit.

We ask for a client's address up to 3 times total, following up after 2 days each time. The final follow-up message can be archived if there's no response.

Nourish Up (NU) Referrals

Some pantries, like Harrison United Methodist Church – Nourish Up, require a referral created through Fishnet.

  • Check Internal Notes for a date of birth. If it's there, the client already has a Fishnet account.
  • Look them up on Fishnet using their DOB to check for an active referral.
  • If they still have refills available, send the “NU Referral – still has refills” template.
  • If their last referral was more than 6 months ago, or they have no DOB on file (meaning no referral has been created), send the “NU Referral Request” template so they can fill out the form and we can set up an appointment.
Example: checking a client's DOB against Fishnet to see if their NU referral still has refills, before deciding which template to send.

Unclear or Off-Topic Messages

For random or unclear messages, send the “How can I help” template. Feel free to translate or adjust as needed, for example:

Hola. Soy Mia de la linea de ayuda alimentaria de Lemontree. Disculpe, no entiendo muy bien su mensaje ¿Cómo le puedo ayudar?

If a client asks something outside our scope (like a replacement ID card or food safety questions), be warm, acknowledge what they shared, be clear that we help with food resources specifically, and point them to the right outside resource when there is one (like USCIS for green card replacement).

For meat or poultry expiration/food safety questions, we don't give food safety advice directly — a template for this is in progress, so check with Alma if you're unsure how to respond in the meantime.

Also in the Official Docs (Lemontree Docs site)

A few things that came up when cross-checking this cheat sheet against the official Specialist Training Manual and related docs — worth knowing even though they didn't come up in the Slack thread this guide was built from:

Scale mismatch to double-check: the official docs describe the “Low Promoter Score” flag as a 0–3 rating, but the example in our Slack thread showed a client responding “1/10” to a recommend-to-a-friend question. Worth confirming with Sam or whoever owns that flag whether there are two different survey scales in play, or whether one source is out of date.
  • Claims on a client last 1 hour and expire automatically — no need to manually release one if you step away.
  • There's a 15-minute SLA goal on flags (a target, not a hard rule) — flags older than that get priority.
  • Official flag type names, if you ever see them in the system: INCOMING_MESSAGE, MANUAL_ONBOARDING, USER_ESCALATION, FOLLOW_UP, LOW_PROMOTER_SCORE, USER.
  • “Send address nudge” is a separate, more automated flow for brand-new clients with no address at all — the system tracks a first and second nudge and stops after that. This is different from the 6-month reconfirmation flow in this cheat sheet (asking existing clients to reconfirm an address up to 3 times), so don't mix the two up.
  • Raffle tickets can be granted manually from a client's profile with a reason (completing a review, sharing a friend's contact, or just a thoughtful gesture) — not only through “Did Not Help.”
  • Banning and deleting clients are both possible from the profile's danger zone, but rare — check with a senior specialist before doing either if you didn't personally work with that client.
  • There's a “Request channel switch” button on the client profile for flipping someone between SMS and WhatsApp if one isn't delivering.
